Honest answers

The questions pastors actually ask us.

What's the catch with the free audit?
There isn't one, and we understand why you're asking. You get the report, the recommendations and the dashboard whether you hire us or not. We don't put you in an email sequence you can't escape and we don't require a second call. The catch, if you want to call it one, is that some churches read the report and decide they'd rather we handled it. Most don't, and that's fine.
We already have someone who does our books.
Good, most churches that call us do. The real question is whether this is that person's gift, or whether it landed on them because nobody else would do it. If it's their gift, we're probably not the right call. If it landed on them, it's worth asking what happens the month they can't. The audit will tell you honestly whether what you have is working.
Can't our CPA just handle the books?
A CPA files your returns and handles compliance. That's a different job from closing your books monthly, tracking designated funds, running clergy payroll and telling you what's happening in your giving. Most CPAs would rather you came to them with clean books, and most charge considerably more per hour to clean them up. Your audit includes a one-page breakdown of what a CPA does versus what we do, so you stop paying twice for the same thing.
Our books are badly behind. Is that a problem?
It's the normal starting point. We've begun with churches two years behind. Cleanup up to twelve months back is included at no charge with a twelve-month agreement, and the audit will tell you exactly how far behind you actually are, which is usually less frightening than it feels.
How long does switching take, and how much of it lands on me?
Under three hours of your time across the first 60 days, and we guarantee it in writing. We contact your current bookkeeper, request the files and handle the handoff conversation. You're not in the middle of it. There's no gap in the books, and the month you switch closes on time like every other month.
How fast do you respond? We've been burned before.
Same day, every day, including Sunday. It's a written guarantee: if we ever go a full business day without a real human answer, that month is free. You get one named Stewardship Partner who knows your church, not a shared inbox and not a rotating support queue.
How do we know we can trust you with this?
Four guarantees in your engagement letter with credits applied automatically, so you never have to chase us for one. A bill approval workflow so no single person, including us, can move money alone. Named church references you can call before you sign anything. And a free audit first, so you can watch how we work before any money changes hands.
What does it cost after the audit?
It depends on your size and what you actually need, which is exactly what the audit determines. What we can tell you now is the comparison that matters: churches consistently find this costs less than a part-time finance hire and delivers things a part-time hire wouldn't produce. You'll have a real number after the audit, with no obligation attached to it.
