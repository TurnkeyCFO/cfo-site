Honesty about the number

What we need to know to lock the price.

The ranges are ranges because we have not seen inside everything yet. We would rather show you the questions than pad the number to cover what we do not know. Answer these and the price becomes fixed.

How far does the site rebuild go?

This is the single question behind the $1,250 to $3,000 range. Answering it takes about five minutes and fixes the number on the spot.

Refresh the existing pages, or rebuild the structure? A refresh keeps the current layout and swaps the message. A rebuild rethinks what pages exist at all. That is most of the difference in price.
How many pages, and which ones survive? There are fourteen live today, including one left over from an old template.
New copy, or reuse what is written? If we write it, that is time. If you write it, that is your deadline.
New photography or video? We do not shoot it, but we plan around what exists rather than discovering the gap in week three.
Are you selling golf and concert tickets on the site, or linking out? Linking out is the low end of the range. Real ticketing, payment and registration is the high end. Nothing else moves the number this much.
Is Camp 75 in scope? It runs on a separate platform with its own subscription. Bringing it in means one less bill and one less place to log in.
Which pages must your team be able to edit themselves? Editable pages take longer to build and save you far more later.
Does the building campaign content stay, move, or get archived? It is most of the current site.

One honest word about the ninety day goal.

Growing 20 to 30 percent in ninety days means finding several hundred new people by late October. Awareness advertising alone will not do that, and anyone who tells you it will is selling. What paid awareness does well is fill the top of the funnel. What converts a visitor into a person who stays is the second and third visit, and that is an engagement problem, which is exactly what Track One measures. We would rather tell you that now than explain it in November.
