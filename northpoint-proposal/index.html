One honest word about the ninety day goal.

Growing 20 to 30 percent in ninety days on your current attendance means finding several hundred new people by late October. Awareness advertising alone will not do that, and anyone who tells you it will is selling. What paid awareness does well is fill the top of the funnel. What actually converts a visitor into a person who stays is the second and third visit, and that is an engagement problem, which is exactly what Track One measures. We would rather tell you that now than explain it in November.
