Project three

The For Austin site.

Yes, and it is well within reach before the fall. The honest answer is that the price and the schedule both hinge on one decision: whether we refresh what is there or rebuild the structure around where the church is going now.

What we would do

  • Rebuild it around the strategy rather than the building campaign
  • Real pages for For Weekend: golf, the concert, For Day, Sunday
  • Your team edits content without waiting on a developer
  • Considerably faster, and the search basics fixed properly
  • It replaces what you pay to host and maintain the site today
  • Live before the fall push, with the deadline agreed up front

What we need to settle first

Refresh, or rebuild? A refresh keeps the layout and changes the message. A rebuild rethinks which pages exist at all. This is most of the difference in both time and cost.
Ticketing on the site, or linking out? Selling golf and concert tickets on your own site is a different piece of work from pointing at an existing registration page. Nothing else moves the number as much.
Who writes the words, and where are the photos? If you have them, we move fast. If we write them, that is time worth planning for rather than discovering in week three.
Does Camp 75 come along? It is on its own platform with its own bill. Bringing it in means one less subscription and one less place to log in.
Your email is never touched.

Email runs on records that are entirely separate from a website. We do not go near them, and nothing in this work can interrupt a single message.

Project four

Growing awareness in the next ninety days.

Also possible, and the piece where we would rather be straight with you than impressive. Paid awareness fills the top of the funnel well. What turns a visitor into someone who stays is the second and third visit, which is exactly what the engagement work measures. The two belong together.

What we would run

  • Landing pages built to turn a click into an actual visit
  • Tracking that ties an ad to a person who showed up, not to a click
  • Campaign build, management, and a weekly read on the dashboard
  • The ninety day goal measured honestly, in the open, every week
  • We do not produce video or photography
  • Your comms team is better at creative than we would be

Ad spend is paid by you directly to the platform. We never mark up media, and you own every account.

What we need to settle first

What is the monthly budget? Below a floor, paid awareness does not move a number anyone can see. Above it, this gets interesting quickly.
What counts as a win? A form, a first visit, a check-in. Pick one and we will measure it honestly, including the weeks it does not work.
Where, exactly? Leander, Cedar Park and Georgetown behave very differently, and the money should follow that.
Is there an ad account and pixel already? It decides whether we start from your own history or from zero.
One honest word on the ninety day goal.

Growing twenty to thirty percent by late October means finding several hundred new people. Advertising alone will not do that, and anyone who says otherwise is selling. What it can do is put far more people in front of the invitation, and then the engagement work tells you which of them stayed. We would rather say that now than explain it in November.
