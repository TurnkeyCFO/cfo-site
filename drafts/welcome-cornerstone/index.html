The first month is about three things: getting connected, getting your history cleaned up, and getting your monthly rhythm started. Here is how it unfolds.

1
Late July

Kickoff

Ricky and Vanessa sit down with Dan and your leadership, in person if we can make it work. We will walk the plan for all three campuses, confirm who owns what on your side, and answer every question before a single entry moves.

Access and setup

We connect to the systems your church already uses: QuickBooks Online, Gusto, Expensify, Planning Center, Overflow, and view-only access to your bank accounts. What we need from you

Your part is small but important. These items unlock the cleanup. The sooner they land, the sooner your books are current.

The QuickBooks connection click. One consent click from an admin connects your books to our reporting. Vanessa will send the link and a short walkthrough video.

System access. Admin invitations to Gusto, Expensify, Planning Center, and Overflow, plus view-only bank access for Chase and AMEX.

Statements. Edward Jones statements back to the account opening, LCRA escrow statements, and documentation for the certificate of deposit.

W-9s. Completed W-9 forms for your musicians and security contractors across all campuses, so January 1099s are painless.

A few answers at kickoff. Your legal entity name for filings, sales tax history, housing allowance designations, and the restriction rules for your named funds.

Answers to our batched questions. During cleanup we will send question lists in batches. Quick turnarounds keep the cleanup moving.
