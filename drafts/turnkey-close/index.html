Why it works when others slip

The deadline is not a hope. It is the way the system is built. Three mechanics make the difference.

One checklist, named owners

Every client has one documented close checklist, run every month by named people, never "whoever gets to it." When a step has an owner, it gets done.

Every transaction touched twice

Coded once, then independently reviewed by a second set of eyes before the close locks. Errors get caught inside the system, not by you.

Deadlines are structural

The close calendar is built into our operating system, not into anyone's memory. The date arrives, and the work is already done.
