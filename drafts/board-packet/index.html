The Quarter in Three Sentences

Giving finished the quarter 6.6% under budget, but June recovered strongly after a soft April, and year-to-date giving remains within a healthy range of plan.

Spending stayed disciplined: total expenses came in 2.1% under budget, with the only meaningful overage in Facilities, driven by a one-time HVAC repair.

06

What Deserves the Board's Attention

  • Summer giving dip is on the calendar. July and August historically run 12 to 15% below the monthly average. Runway comfortably absorbs it, but any new spending commitments are best deferred to September.
  • The Building Fund is approaching its first milestone. At the current pace it crosses $175,000 in early Q3, the threshold the board set for engaging an architect. A decision item should be on the September agenda.
  • Benevolence requests are rising. Fund outflow ran ahead of inflow this quarter. The fund is healthy today, but the board may want to schedule a special offering this fall to stay ahead of demand.
