What changed this month

Plain English, from your bookkeeping team. No jargon, no spreadsheet homework.

  • Revenue hit a six month high. July came in at $96,400, up 7.1% from June. The new catering line drove most of the gain and now makes up about 9% of sales.
  • The June dip was weather, not a trend. Two storm weekends cut foot traffic in June. July's bounce back confirms the underlying trajectory is still up and to the right.
  • Watch equipment costs. The espresso machine at the Lamar location needed a second repair in three months, putting that line 45% over budget. It may be cheaper to replace than keep fixing. Worth a decision before August.
  • Marketing overage looks intentional. The overage traces to the summer event sponsorships you approved in May. If those stay, we suggest raising that budget line rather than flagging it every month.
  • Cash keeps building. Runway grew to 11.4 weeks. You are in a comfortable position to fund the patio buildout from cash if you choose to.
