03

The monthly rhythm

Every month runs on the same calendar of business days. You always know where your books stand, without asking.

First business day
One short email with anything we need from you to close the prior month. One list, not a drip.
First week
Transactions coded, all three bank accounts and the card reconciled, questions batched to you in a single email.
Mid-month
Books finalized and locked, reviewed by a second set of eyes before anything reaches you.
Reports delivered
Your profit and loss and balance sheet, in plain English: what changed, what to watch, and what we already handled.

Also on the standing calendar: 1099 preparation each January for your crew and vendors, and sales tax support as we confirm what is taxable across your fabrication and installation work.

04

What we need from you

Short list. Everything here can happen over email, and none of it takes you off a job site for long.

The signed engagement letter. Sign and return, and confirm the legal name question (Inc. versus LLC) so your documents all match.

Loan and equipment statements. Statements for the truck loan and any financed equipment, so what you owe shows accurately on the books.

A quick flag on personal spend. A few purchases look like they may be personal rather than shop. A one-line answer per item keeps your tax picture clean.

W-9s for the crew. Current W-9 forms for everyone paid as a contractor, so January 1099s are painless.

Your CPA's contact. An introduction to whoever is preparing the 2025 return, so the book-to-tax handoff is seamless in September.

Email replies to batched questions. We know email is how you work. We batch our questions; quick replies keep everything on schedule.
