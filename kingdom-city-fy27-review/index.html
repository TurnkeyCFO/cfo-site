02 — How the church is funded

More than half of FY27 income still comes from outside.

Kingdom City is funded by its own people — tithes plus the Vision Fund's designated giving — and by a schedule of partner churches. The plan deliberately lets that outside support fall away as member giving climbs — so each year the church stands more firmly on its own. Today internal giving is 46% of income and external support 54%; by FY29 external is just 21%.

03 — The giving engine

Two assumptions drive the entire plan.

Everything on this page rests on two levers: how many people gather, and how much each one gives. Attendance rises from 140 to 162 across FY27, and giving per attendee nearly doubles from $45 to $86.25 a month — pushing toward Blake's $90 target by July 2027 and $160 by June 2029. Move either lever and every number moves with it.

The team behind the budget.

07 — The month by month

The year does not fund itself evenly.

Income rises steadily every month while expenses spike in the fall. September is the one deep-dip month — the fall ministry and outreach season lands all at once — with October roughly breakeven and December slightly negative. The other nine months carry the year comfortably to a full-year surplus.

The guidance

A sane first-ever reveal.

  1. Sunday morning, Sep 14 → Level 1 or 2. Percentages tell the self-sustaining story without a single church dollar figure on screen. Westridge's own precedent is Level 2.
  2. Heart & Soul Night → Level 3 or 4. This room — staff and ministry partners — funds the budget and has earned the dollars.
  3. The recommended pairing: Level 2 on Sunday, Level 3 at Heart & Soul, with Level 4 available on request — printed, not projected.
  4. Whatever the level, always land on the same ask: $45 → $90 per person, per month — the one step that carries the church to standing fully on its own.
